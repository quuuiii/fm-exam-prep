Chapter 1 · Introduction to Corporate Finance第 1 章 · 公司理財導論

1.1 Five Basic Areas of Finance 財務的五大領域

  • Corporate finance — how firms raise and invest money (the focus of this course).公司理財：企業如何籌資與投資（本課程重點）。
  • Investments — financial assets such as stocks and bonds: pricing, risk and return, portfolio management.投資學：股票、債券等金融資產的評價、風險報酬與投資組合管理。
  • Financial institutions — banks, insurers, securities firms and other intermediaries.金融機構：銀行、保險公司、證券商等金融中介。
  • International finance — exchange rates, cross-border investment and financing.國際財務：匯率、跨國投資與融資。
  • Real estate and related asset markets.不動產及相關資產市場。
  • Course example: a financial holding company such as Cathay FHC spans several areas at once (banking, insurance, securities, investment trust).課堂案例：國泰金控同時橫跨多個領域（銀行、保險、證券、投信）。

1.2 Three Financial Management Decisions 三大財務管理決策

  • Capital budgeting — which long-term investments (projects, fixed assets) should the firm make? Evaluate the size, timing and risk of future cash flows.資本預算：公司應投資哪些長期資產或專案？評估未來現金流量的大小、時點與風險。
  • Capital structure — how should the firm finance those investments: what mix of long-term debt and equity, and from which sources?資本結構：如何為投資籌資？長期負債與權益的比例及資金來源為何？
  • Working capital management — managing short-term assets and liabilities (cash, inventory, receivables, payables) so day-to-day operations are never short of cash.營運資金管理：管理短期資產與負債（現金、存貨、應收、應付帳款），確保日常營運不缺現金。
  • Who decides: the CFO oversees the treasurer (cash, capital budgeting, financing) and the controller (accounting, taxes, cost accounting).誰負責：財務長 (CFO) 督導財務主管（現金、資本預算、融資）與會計主管（會計、稅務、成本會計）。

  • Key idea: in a corporation, ownership is separated from management — shareholders elect the board, the board hires managers. This is what makes the agency problem possible.關鍵觀念：公司制下所有權與經營權分離——股東選董事會、董事會聘經理人，這正是代理問題的根源。
  • Hybrids: a Limited Liability Company (LLC) gives limited liability like a corporation but is taxed like a partnership.混合型：有限責任公司 (LLC) 像公司一樣負有限責任，卻像合夥一樣課稅。

1.4 The Goal of Financial Management 財務管理的目標

  • Goal: maximize the current value per share of the existing stock — equivalently, maximize the market value of owners' equity.目標：極大化現有股票的每股目前價值——即極大化股東權益的市場價值。
  • Why not "maximize profit"? Profit is ambiguous (this year or long run? accounting or cash?), ignores the timing and risk of cash flows. Stock price captures all three.為何不是「利潤極大化」？利潤定義模糊（今年或長期？會計或現金？），且忽略現金流量的時點與風險；股價則三者兼顧。
  • Shareholders are residual claimants: they are paid only after employees, suppliers and creditors. If equity value is rising, everyone else has been paid.股東是剩餘請求權人：員工、供應商、債權人先領，股東最後。若權益價值上升，代表其他人都已獲得支付。
  • Sarbanes-Oxley Act (2002) strengthened protection against corporate fraud (e.g., Enron, WorldCom) by requiring management to certify financial statements.沙賓法案 (2002)：因安隆、世界通訊等弊案而立法，要求管理階層對財報簽證負責，強化防弊。

1.5 The Agency Problem 代理問題

  • Agency relationship: a principal (shareholders) hires an agent (managers) to act on its behalf. Agency problem: conflict of interest between them.代理關係：主理人（股東）委託代理人（經理人）代為行事；代理問題即兩者的利益衝突。
  • Main cause: dispersed ownership — no single shareholder has enough stake to monitor, so management effectively controls the firm.主因：股權結構分散——無單一股東有足夠誘因監督，經理人實質控制公司。
  • Agency costs: indirect = lost opportunities (e.g., managers reject a good but risky project to protect their jobs); direct = (1) corporate spending that benefits managers (corporate jet), (2) monitoring costs (outside auditors).代理成本：間接成本＝錯失的機會（如經理人為保飯碗拒絕高風險但有價值的專案）；直接成本＝(1) 圖利經理人的支出（公務機），(2) 監督成本（外部稽核）。
  • Compensation matters: paying a car-selling agent a flat fee rewards a quick sale; a 10% commission aligns her with getting the best price.報酬設計很重要：委託他人賣車若付固定費用，代理人只想快點賣掉；改付 10% 佣金，才會盡力賣到好價錢。
  • Control mechanisms: performance-based pay and stock options; promotion prospects; shareholders' power to elect the board; proxy fights; takeover threats (poorly run firms are acquisition targets).控制機制：績效獎酬與員工認股權；升遷前景；股東選舉董事權；委託書爭奪戰；接管威脅（經營不善的公司易被併購）。
  • Stakeholders (employees, customers, suppliers, government) also have claims on the firm's cash flows.利害關係人（員工、顧客、供應商、政府）對公司現金流量也有請求權。

1.6 Financial Markets 金融市場

  • Cash flow cycle: firm sells securities → receives cash → invests in assets → assets generate cash → pays taxes, retains some, pays dividends/interest to investors.現金流循環：公司發行證券 → 取得現金 → 投資資產 → 資產產生現金 → 繳稅、保留盈餘、支付股利／利息給投資人。
  • Primary market: the original sale of securities by the issuer (IPO, seasoned offering); the firm receives the money. Secondary market: investors trade among themselves; the firm receives nothing.初級市場：發行公司首次出售證券（IPO、現金增資），公司取得資金。次級市場：投資人彼此交易，公司不收取資金。
  • Public offering (registered with the SEC, sold to the public) vs private placement (sold to a few institutions).公開發行（向 SEC 註冊、賣給大眾）vs 私募（賣給少數機構投資人）。
  • Dealer market (dealers buy/sell from own inventory, e.g., Nasdaq, OTC) vs auction market (buyers matched with sellers in one place, e.g., NYSE).自營商市場（自營商以自有存貨買賣，如 Nasdaq、櫃買）vs 競價市場（買賣雙方集中撮合，如 NYSE）。
  • Money market (debt maturing < 1 year) vs capital market (long-term debt and equity).貨幣市場（到期日 < 1 年的債務工具）vs 資本市場（長期債務與股票）。

Chapter 2 · Financial Statements, Taxes and Cash Flow第 2 章 · 財務報表、稅與現金流量

2.1 The Balance Sheet 資產負債表

  • A snapshot of what the firm owns and owes at a point in time.某一時點公司擁有與積欠的快照。
  • Assets listed in order of decreasing liquidity: current assets (cash, A/R, inventory) then fixed assets (tangible: plant & equipment; intangible: patents, trademarks).資產依流動性由高到低排列：流動資產（現金、應收帳款、存貨），再到固定資產（有形：廠房設備；無形：專利、商標）。
  • Liabilities in order of when due: current liabilities (< 1 yr: A/P, notes payable) then long-term debt.負債依到期先後排列：流動負債（1 年內：應付帳款、應付票據），再到長期負債。
Balance Sheet Identity 資產負債表恆等式
Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity
AssetsEverything the firm owns · 公司擁有的一切資源
LiabilitiesWhat the firm owes to creditors · 對債權人的負債
SEResidual owners' claim = Assets − Liabilities · 股東剩餘請求權
Intuition 直覺：Equity is defined as the residual, so the identity always holds. If asset values fall below liabilities, equity is negative (the firm is insolvent on a book basis).
權益是「剩餘」，因此恆等式必定成立。若資產低於負債，帳面權益為負（帳面上無力償債）。
Net Working Capital 淨營運資金
NWC = Current Assets − Current Liabilities
CAAssets convertible to cash within 1 year · 一年內可變現資產
CLObligations due within 1 year · 一年內到期負債
Intuition 直覺：Positive NWC means cash coming in over the next 12 months exceeds cash that must be paid out — a short-term liquidity cushion.
NWC 為正代表未來 12 個月可收現的金額大於需支付的金額——短期流動性的緩衝。
  • Liquidity: how quickly and easily an asset converts to cash without significant loss of value. More liquidity = less likely to face financial distress, but liquid assets earn lower returns (trade-off).流動性：資產能多快、多容易轉為現金且不大幅折價。流動性越高越不易陷入財務危機，但報酬較低（取捨）。
  • Debt vs equity / financial leverage: using debt magnifies both gains and losses to shareholders and increases the risk of financial distress.負債 vs 權益／財務槓桿：舉債會放大股東的利得與損失，並提高財務危機風險。
  • Market value vs book value: book value = historical cost (GAAP); market value = what assets would sell for today. Managers and investors care about market value; the balance sheet often omits key assets (good management, brand, people).市價 vs 帳面價值：帳面價值＝歷史成本 (GAAP)；市價＝今天能賣得的價格。經理人與投資人重視市價；資產負債表常漏掉重要資產（好的管理團隊、品牌、人才）。

2.2 The Income Statement 損益表

  • Measures performance over a period (like a video, versus the balance-sheet snapshot).衡量一段期間的經營績效（像錄影，相對於資產負債表的快照）。
Income Statement Equation 損益表方程式
Revenues − Expenses = Net Income
Sales − COGS − Depreciation = EBIT;  EBIT − Interest = EBT;  EBT − Taxes = NI
COGSCost of goods sold · 銷貨成本
EBITEarnings before interest & taxes (operating income) · 息前稅前盈餘（營業利益）
EBTEarnings before taxes · 稅前盈餘
NINet income = Dividends + Addition to retained earnings · 淨利＝股利＋保留盈餘增加數
EPSNI ÷ shares outstanding · 每股盈餘
Intuition 直覺：EBIT shows how well the operations perform, before financing (interest) and government (taxes) take their shares.
EBIT 反映本業表現，尚未扣除融資成本（利息）與政府分配（稅）。
  • GAAP — Recognition (realization) principle: revenue is recorded when earned (goods delivered), not necessarily when cash is collected. Matching principle: expenses are recorded against the revenues they produce.GAAP 認列（實現）原則：收入於賺得時（商品交付）入帳，不一定是收到現金時。配合原則：費用與其所產生的收入在同期認列。
  • Noncash items: depreciation is an expense that reduces NI but involves no cash outflow — so NI ≠ cash flow.非現金項目：折舊會減少淨利，但沒有現金流出——因此淨利 ≠ 現金流量。
  • Time and costs: in the short run some costs are fixed; in the long run all costs are variable. Product costs (raw materials, direct labor) go into COGS; period costs (SG&A) are expensed in the period.時間與成本：短期部分成本固定，長期所有成本皆變動。產品成本（原料、直接人工）列入銷貨成本；期間成本（銷管費用）於當期認列。

2.3 Taxes 稅負 TEXTBOOK

Average vs Marginal Tax Rate 平均稅率 vs 邊際稅率
Average rate = Total tax ÷ Taxable income
Marginal rate = tax paid on the next $1 earned
Intuition 直覺：Financial decisions use the marginal rate because new projects generate additional income. Since 2018 the U.S. federal corporate rate is a flat 21%, so average = marginal for corporations.
財務決策應使用邊際稅率，因為新專案產生的是額外所得。美國自 2018 年起聯邦公司稅為單一 21%，故公司的平均稅率＝邊際稅率。

2.4 Cash Flow 現金流量 TEXTBOOK

Cash Flow Identity 現金流量恆等式
CF(A) = OCF − NCS − ΔNWC = CF to creditors + CF to stockholders
CF(A)Cash flow from assets (free cash flow) · 資產現金流量（自由現金流量）
OCFOperating cash flow = EBIT + Depreciation − Taxes · 營運現金流量
NCSNet capital spending = Ending net FA − Beginning net FA + Depreciation · 淨資本支出
ΔNWCEnding NWC − Beginning NWC · 淨營運資金變動
CF to creditorsInterest paid − Net new borrowing · 對債權人現金流量
CF to stockholdersDividends paid − Net new equity raised · 對股東現金流量
Intuition 直覺：Cash the assets generate (after reinvesting in fixed assets and working capital) must equal cash paid to the firm's suppliers of capital. Negative CF(A) is not necessarily bad — a fast-growing firm invests heavily.
資產產生的現金（扣除固定資產與營運資金的再投資後）必等於支付給資金提供者的現金。CF(A) 為負不一定是壞事——高成長公司正大量投資。

Chapter 4 · Introduction to Valuation: The Time Value of Money第 4 章 · 評價導論：貨幣的時間價值

4.1 Core Vocabulary 核心名詞

  • Time value of money (TVM): a dollar today is worth more than a dollar later, because today's dollar can be invested to earn interest.貨幣時間價值：今天的一元比未來的一元更有價值，因為今天的錢可以投資賺取利息。
  • Compounding = growing a value forward (PV → FV). Discounting = bringing a future value back (FV → PV).複利＝把價值往未來推（PV → FV）。折現＝把未來價值拉回今天（FV → PV）。
  • Simple interest is earned only on the original principal. Compound interest also earns interest on interest.單利只對原始本金計息。複利還會產生利上加利。

4.2 Future Value 終值

Future Value (single sum) 單筆終值
FV = PV × (1 + r)t
FVFuture value at time t · 第 t 期的終值
PVPresent value (amount invested today) · 現值（今天投入金額）
rInterest rate per period · 每期利率
tNumber of periods · 期數
(1+r)tFuture value interest factor (FVIF) · 終值利率因子
Intuition 直覺：Each period the balance is multiplied by (1+r); growth is exponential, so long horizons and high rates have dramatic effects.
每期餘額乘上 (1+r)，呈指數成長；期間越長、利率越高，效果越驚人。
Worked Example 範例
Invest $100 at 10% for 5 years. FV = 100 × 1.15 = 100 × 1.61051 = $161.05. Simple interest would give 100 + 5 × 10 = $150, so interest-on-interest = $11.05. 投資 $100，年利率 10%，5 年。FV = 100 × 1.15 = $161.05。單利僅得 100 + 5×10 = $150，故利上加利 = $11.05。

4.3 Present Value 現值

Present Value (single sum) 單筆現值
PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)t = FV × [1 / (1 + r)t]
rDiscount rate · 折現率
1/(1+r)tDiscount factor (PVIF) · 折現因子
Intuition 直覺：PV answers "how much must I invest today to have FV later?" PV falls as r rises or t lengthens — the inverse relationship between rate and value underlies bond pricing.
PV 回答「今天要投入多少，未來才能得到 FV？」利率越高或期間越長，PV 越低——這種反向關係正是債券評價的基礎。
Worked Example 範例（課堂）
How much to invest today at 10% to get $1 in one year? PV × 1.1 = $1 → PV = 1/1.1 = $0.909. 年利率 10%，一年後要拿到 $1，今天需投入多少？PV × 1.1 = $1 → PV = $0.909。

4.4 Solving for the Rate and the Number of Periods 求解利率與期數

Discount Rate 求報酬率
r = (FV / PV)1/t − 1
Intuition 直覺：The compound annual growth rate that turns PV into FV in t periods.
讓 PV 在 t 期後成長為 FV 的年複合成長率。
Number of Periods 求期數
t = ln(FV / PV) ÷ ln(1 + r)
Rule of 72 (72 法則): years to double ≈ 72 ÷ (r × 100). At 8%, money doubles in about 9 years.
資金翻倍所需年數 ≈ 72 ÷ 利率(%)。年利 8% 約 9 年翻倍。
Worked Example 範例
$5,000 grows to $10,000 in 6 years → r = 21/6 − 1 = 12.25%. At 10%, how long to turn $5,000 into $10,000? t = ln 2 / ln 1.1 = 7.27 years. $5,000 在 6 年後成長為 $10,000 → r = 21/6 − 1 = 12.25%。利率 10% 時，$5,000 變 $10,000 需 t = ln2/ln1.1 = 7.27 年。

4.5 Using the Financial Calculator 財務計算機操作

  • Five TVM keys: N (periods), I/Y (rate in %, not decimal), PV, PMT (level payment), FV. Enter any four, compute (CPT) the fifth.五個 TVM 鍵：N（期數）、I/Y（利率，輸入百分比而非小數）、PV、PMT（每期等額付款）、FV。輸入任四個，按 CPT 求第五個。
  • Sign convention: cash outflows are negative, inflows positive. When solving for r or t, PV and FV must have opposite signs or the calculator returns an error.正負號慣例：現金流出為負、流入為正。求 r 或 t 時，PV 與 FV 必須一正一負，否則計算機會顯示錯誤。
  • Always clear the TVM registers (2nd → CLR TVM) and check P/Y = 1 before a new problem.每題前先清除 TVM 暫存（2nd → CLR TVM），並確認 P/Y = 1。

Chapter 5 · Discounted Cash Flow Valuation第 5 章 · 現金流量折現評價

5.1 Multiple Cash Flows 多筆現金流量

PV and FV of Uneven Cash Flows 不等額現金流量的現值與終值
PV = Σ Ct / (1 + r)t    FVT = Σ Ct × (1 + r)T − t
CtCash flow at end of period t · 第 t 期末現金流量
THorizon date for FV · 終值的目標時點
Intuition 直覺：Value is additive. Move every cash flow to the same date, then add. Two methods for FV: compound the running balance forward year by year, or compound each cash flow separately and sum.
價值具可加性：把每筆現金流移到同一時點再相加。求終值可逐年累積餘額，或各筆分別複利後加總。
Worked Example 範例
Receive $200 in year 1 and $400 in year 2; r = 12%. PV = 200/1.12 + 400/1.122 = 178.57 + 318.88 = $497.45. 第 1 年收 $200、第 2 年收 $400，r = 12%。PV = 178.57 + 318.88 = $497.45。
  • Timing assumption: unless stated, cash flows occur at the end of each period.時點假設：除非特別說明，現金流量皆發生在每期期末。

5.2 Annuities and Perpetuities 年金與永續年金

Present Value of an Ordinary Annuity 普通年金現值
PV = C × [1 − 1/(1 + r)t] / r
CLevel payment each period (end of period) · 每期期末等額支付
rRate per period · 每期利率
tNumber of payments · 支付次數
[ ]/rAnnuity PV factor · 年金現值因子
Intuition 直覺：An annuity = a perpetuity starting now (C/r) minus a perpetuity starting at t (its PV is (C/r)/(1+r)t). That is exactly the formula.
年金 = 從現在開始的永續年金 (C/r) 減去從第 t 期才開始的永續年金（其現值為 (C/r)/(1+r)t），即得此公式。
Future Value of an Ordinary Annuity 普通年金終值
FV = C × [(1 + r)t − 1] / r
Intuition 直覺：Equals PV of the annuity × (1+r)t. Used for retirement savings: how much will regular deposits grow to?
等於年金現值 × (1+r)t。常用於退休儲蓄：定期存款最後會累積多少？
Annuity Due 期初年金
PVdue = PVordinary × (1 + r)    FVdue = FVordinary × (1 + r)
Intuition 直覺：Payments come at the beginning of each period (rent, leases), so each payment is one period earlier → worth (1+r) times more. Calculator: set BGN mode.
支付在每期期初（如房租、租賃），每筆都早一期，價值多乘 (1+r)。計算機設定 BGN 模式。
Perpetuity 永續年金
PV = C / r
CLevel payment forever, first at end of period 1 · 永久等額支付，第 1 期末開始
Intuition 直覺：If you deposit C/r at rate r, interest each year is exactly C and the principal is never touched. Example: preferred stock (consol). $1 per year forever at 8% → PV = $12.50.
若存入 C/r、利率 r，每年利息恰為 C 且本金永不動用。例：特別股（永續債）。每年 $1、利率 8% → PV = $12.50。
Growing Perpetuity and Growing Annuity 成長型永續年金與成長年金
Growing perpetuity: PV = C1 / (r − g),  r > g
Growing annuity: PV = C1 / (r − g) × [1 − ((1 + g)/(1 + r))t]
C1First cash flow, at end of period 1 · 第 1 期現金流量
gConstant growth rate of cash flows · 現金流量固定成長率
Intuition 直覺：Growth makes later payments larger, raising value; the formula requires r > g or value is infinite. This is the basis of the dividend growth model for stocks (Ch 7).
成長使後期支付變大、價值提高；必須 r > g，否則價值無限大。此為股票股利成長模型（第 7 章）的基礎。

5.3 Solving for Payment, Rate and Periods 求解付款額、利率與期數

Annuity Payment (loan payment) 年金每期付款（貸款攤還額）
C = PV × r / [1 − 1/(1 + r)t]
Intuition 直覺：Rearranging the annuity PV formula: the level payment that exactly pays off a loan of PV over t periods.
由年金現值公式移項：在 t 期內剛好還清 PV 貸款的每期等額付款。
  • Finding r for an annuity has no closed-form solution: use trial and error or the calculator (N, PV, PMT → CPT I/Y).年金求 r 沒有公式解：需試誤法或計算機（輸入 N、PV、PMT → CPT I/Y）。
  • Finding t: how long to pay off a credit card balance with fixed payments — use the calculator (I/Y, PV, PMT → CPT N).求 t：固定還款多久能還清卡債——用計算機（I/Y、PV、PMT → CPT N）。

5.4 Comparing Rates: APR vs EAR 利率比較：名目年利率 vs 有效年利率

Effective Annual Rate 有效年利率
EAR = (1 + APR/m)m − 1
APRAnnual percentage rate = quoted (stated) rate = rate per period × m · 名目（報價）年利率
mCompounding periods per year · 每年複利次數
EARRate actually earned/paid after compounding · 考慮複利後實際的年利率
Intuition 直覺：Quoted rates are not comparable when compounding differs. Convert everything to EAR, then pick the highest for investing or lowest for borrowing. EAR ≥ APR; more frequent compounding → higher EAR.
複利次數不同時報價利率無法直接比較；全部換算成 EAR 後，投資選最高、借款選最低。EAR ≥ APR，複利越頻繁 EAR 越高。
Converting EAR to APR and Continuous Compounding EAR 換算 APR 與連續複利
APR = m × [(1 + EAR)1/m − 1]    EARcontinuous = eAPR − 1
Intuition 直覺：Continuous compounding is the upper limit as m → ∞.
連續複利是 m → ∞ 的上限。
Worked Example 範例
Bank A: 15.0% compounded daily; Bank B: 15.5% compounded quarterly; Bank C: 16% annually. EARA = (1+.15/365)365−1 = 16.18%; EARB = (1+.155/4)4−1 = 16.42%; EARC = 16.00%. As a saver, choose B; as a borrower, choose C. A 銀行 15% 日複利；B 銀行 15.5% 季複利；C 銀行 16% 年複利。EAR 分別為 16.18%、16.42%、16.00%。存款選 B，借款選 C。
  • Truth-in-Lending laws require lenders to disclose the APR — but APR understates true cost when compounding is more than annual.誠實借貸法要求揭露 APR——但當複利多於一年一次時，APR 會低估真實成本。

5.5 Loan Types and Amortization 貸款類型與攤還

  • Pure discount loan: borrower receives money today and repays one lump sum later (e.g., T-bills). PV = F/(1+r)t.純折價貸款：今天借款，到期一次還清本息（如國庫券）。PV = F/(1+r)t。
  • Interest-only loan: pay interest each period, repay entire principal at maturity (like most corporate bonds).只付息貸款：每期只付利息，到期一次還本（類似多數公司債）。
  • Amortized loan — fixed principal: repay the same principal each period; interest falls over time, so total payment declines.攤還貸款—本金平均攤還：每期償還相同本金；利息逐期減少，總付款遞減。
  • Amortized loan — fixed payment: equal total payment (annuity), most common (mortgages, car loans). Early payments are mostly interest; later ones mostly principal.攤還貸款—本息平均攤還：每期總付款相同（年金），最常見（房貸、車貸）。前期多為利息，後期多為本金。
Worked Example 範例（課堂）: $5,000, 9%, 5 years
Fixed principal: repay $1,000/yr. Interest = 450, 360, 270, 180, 90 → total interest $1,350.
Fixed payment: C = 5,000 × 0.09 / [1 − 1/1.095] = 450 / 0.35007 = $1,285.46. Total paid = 5 × 1,285.46 = 6,427.31 → total interest $1,427.31.
The fixed-payment loan costs more interest only because principal is repaid more slowly early on — neither loan is "better"; one is simply paid off faster. 本金平均攤還：每年還本 $1,000，利息依序 450、360、270、180、90，總利息 $1,350。
本息平均攤還：C = $1,285.46，總付款 $6,427.31，總利息 $1,427.31。
後者利息較高只是因為前期還本較慢——兩者無優劣之分，只是前者較快還清。
