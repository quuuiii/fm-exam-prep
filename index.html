Chapter 1 · Introduction to Financial Management第 1 章 · 財務管理導論

1.1 Five Basic Areas of Finance 財務的五大領域

  • 1. Corporate finance (= business finance) — the main subject of the course. Many of its ideas apply across all areas of finance, not just corporations.1. 公司理財（＝商業財務）：本課程主軸，其觀念適用於所有財務領域，不限於公司。
  • 2. Investments — financial assets such as stocks and bonds: (1) what determines their price, (2) their risks and returns, (3) the best mix to hold. Careers: stockbroker, financial advisor, portfolio manager, security analyst.2. 投資：股票、債券等金融資產——(1) 價格如何決定、(2) 風險與報酬、(3) 最佳組合。職涯：股票經紀人、財務規劃師、投資組合經理、證券分析師。
  • 3. Financial institutions — businesses dealing mainly in financial matters: banks (commercial, investment, credit unions, savings & loans), insurance companies, brokerage firms.3. 金融機構：以金融業務為主的企業——銀行（商業銀行、投資銀行、信用合作社、儲貸機構）、保險公司、證券經紀商。
  • 4. International finance — a specialization within each area above; requires knowledge of exchange rates, political risk and foreign customs.4. 國際財務：上述各領域中的國際面向，需了解匯率、政治風險與他國習俗。
  • 5. Fintech — firms using the internet, mobile phones, software or cloud services to provide financial services.5. 金融科技：運用網路、手機、軟體或雲端服務提供金融服務的公司。
  • Taiwan examples in class: Cathay FHC, E.Sun FHC and Fubon Bank organization charts — a financial holding company spans several areas at once.課堂台灣案例：國泰金控、玉山金控、富邦銀行組織圖——金控公司同時橫跨多個領域。

1.2 Why Study Finance? 為什麼要學財務？

  • Marketing: budgets, payoff of marketing spending, pricing and distribution decisions all use financial tools; financial analysts rely on marketers to evaluate projects.行銷：預算、行銷支出的效益、定價與通路決策都需財務工具；財務分析師也仰賴行銷分析評估專案。
  • Accounting: accountants in small firms often make financial decisions; financial analysts are major users of accounting information.會計：小公司的會計常需做財務決策；財務分析師是會計資訊最重要的使用者之一。
  • Management: business strategy without financial strategy is "a recipe for disaster"; managers must understand how their jobs affect profitability.管理：只想經營策略而不考慮財務策略，必然失敗；管理者需了解自己的工作如何影響獲利。
  • Technology (Fintech examples): mobile payment (Venmo, PayPal, Line Pay); crowdfunding (Kickstarter, GoFundMe, Zeczec); working-capital lenders for startups (Kabbage, Lendio); P2P lending (Upstart, Prosper); buy now, pay later (Affirm, AFTEE); blockchain & cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, 2009); insurtech (Lemonade, Oscar Health, Trov); robo-advisors and commission-free trading (Robinhood, Acorns); budgeting apps.科技（金融科技例子）：行動支付（Venmo、PayPal、Line Pay）；群眾募資（Kickstarter、GoFundMe、嘖嘖）；新創營運資金融資（Kabbage、Lendio）；P2P 借貸（Upstart、Prosper）；先買後付（Affirm、AFTEE、中租零卡）；區塊鏈與加密貨幣（比特幣，2009）；保險科技（Lemonade、Oscar Health、Trov）；機器人理財與免手續費交易（Robinhood、Acorns）；記帳 App。
  • You: the most important reason — you will make personal financial decisions (retirement, starting a business, student loans, mortgages, investments).你自己：最重要的理由——你一定會面臨個人財務決策（退休、創業、學貸、房貸、投資）。

1.3 Business Finance: Three Questions & the Financial Manager 商業財務三大問題與財務經理

  • Three questions: (1) What long-term investments should the firm take on? (2) Where will we get the long-term financing? (3) How will we manage everyday financial activities?三大問題：(1) 公司應做哪些長期投資？(2) 長期資金從哪裡來？(3) 如何管理日常財務活動？
  • CFO (chief financial officer) coordinates the treasurer (cash management, credit management, capital expenditures, financial planning) and the controller (taxes, cost accounting, financial accounting, data processing).財務長 (CFO) 協調財務主管（現金管理、信用管理、資本支出、財務規劃）與會計主管（稅務、成本會計、財務會計、資料處理）。

1.4 Three Financial Management Decisions 三大財務管理決策

  • Capital budgeting (資本預算) — planning and managing long-term investments; find opportunities worth more than they cost. Essence: the size, timing and risk of future cash flows.資本預算：規劃與管理長期投資；找出價值高於成本的投資機會。核心是未來現金流量的大小、時點與風險。
  • Capital structure (資本結構) — the specific mix of long-term debt and equity. Concerns: how much to borrow, and what are the least expensive sources of funds.資本結構：長期負債與權益的組合。重點：該借多少？最便宜的資金來源是什麼？
  • Working capital management (營運資金管理) — managing short-term assets (inventory) and liabilities (payables to suppliers). Questions: how much cash and inventory to hold? sell on credit? how and where to get short-term financing?營運資金管理：管理短期資產（存貨）與短期負債（應付供應商款項）。問題：持有多少現金與存貨？是否賒銷？短期資金如何、向誰取得？

1.6 The Goal of Financial Management 財務管理的目標

  • Why not profit maximization? Imprecise: this year's profit (deferring maintenance, running down inventory raises it but isn't desirable)? Long-run profit ("in the long run, we're all dead")? Accounting net income or EPS may say little about what is good for the firm, and it gives no trade-off between current and future profits.為何不是利潤極大化？定義不清：今年的利潤（延後維修、降低存貨可提高利潤但不可取）？長期利潤（長期來看我們都死了）？會計淨利或 EPS 不見得反映公司好壞，也無法權衡現在與未來的獲利。
  • Goal: good decisions raise the stock's value, poor ones lower it → maximize the current value per share of the existing stock (股票價值最大化).目標：好決策提升股價、壞決策降低股價 → 極大化現有股票的每股目前價值。
  • If the firm has no traded stock: maximize the market value of the existing owners' equity (權益市值最大化).若公司沒有公開交易的股票：極大化現有股東權益的市場價值。
  • This goal does not imply illegal or unethical actions.此目標不代表可以採取違法或不道德的行為。

1.7 The Agency Problem 代理問題

  • Main cause: dispersion of ownership (股權結構分散) — management may effectively control the firm and pursue its own goals at stockholders' expense.主因：股權結構分散——管理階層實質控制公司，可能犧牲股東利益追求自身目標。
  • Agency relationship: a principal hires an agent to represent his/her interest. A conflict of interest between them = agency problem.代理關係：主理人委託代理人代表其利益；兩者的利益衝突即代理問題。
  • Car example: a flat fee makes the agent want just to sell; a 10% commission makes her seek the best price → compensation affects agency problems.賣車例子：付固定費用，代理人只想賣掉；付 10% 佣金，她才會爭取最高價 → 報酬方式影響代理問題。
  • Agency cost examples: (1) managers reject a valuable but risky project to protect their jobs — shareholders lose the opportunity; (2) managers overemphasize size/growth, e.g., overpaying to acquire another company (over-diversification, price wars for market share).代理成本例子：(1) 經理人為保飯碗拒絕有價值但高風險的專案，股東失去機會；(2) 經理人過度追求規模，如溢價併購他公司（過度多角化、削價搶市占）。
  • Two incentives for managers: Compensation — pay tied to performance and share value, e.g., stock options; Job prospects — better performers get promoted and command higher salaries.促使經理人為股東著想的兩項誘因：報酬——與績效及股價連動，如股票選擇權；職涯前景——表現好者升遷、在人力市場更搶手。
  • Control of the firm: stockholders elect the board, which hires and fires management. Proxy fight — a group solicits proxies (authority to vote others' stock) to replace the board. Takeover — poorly managed firms are attractive acquisition targets.公司控制權：股東選董事會，董事會任免經理人。委託書爭奪戰——徵求委託書（代他人投票的權利）以撤換董事會。接管——經營不善的公司更容易被收購。
  • Stakeholders: employees, customers, suppliers, creditors, government — parties other than stockholders with a potential claim on the firm's cash flows.利害關係人：員工、顧客、供應商、債權人、政府——股東以外對公司現金流量有潛在請求權者。

1.8 Financial Markets and the Corporation 金融市場與公司

  • Figure 1.2 cash flow cycle: (A) firm issues securities to raise cash → (B) invests in current and fixed assets → (C) assets generate cash → (D) taxes to government / other stakeholders → (E) cash reinvested in the firm → (F) interest and dividends paid to investors.圖 1.2 現金流循環：(A) 發行證券籌資 → (B) 投資流動與固定資產 → (C) 資產產生現金 → (D) 繳稅給政府／其他利害關係人 → (E) 再投資回公司 → (F) 支付利息與股利給投資人。
  • Primary market (初級市場): the corporation is the seller and raises money. Public offering — sold to the general public, must register with the SEC (costly disclosure). Private placement — negotiated sale to a specific buyer (e.g., life insurers, mutual funds), no SEC registration or underwriters needed.初級市場：公司是賣方並取得資金。公開發行——賣給大眾，需向 SEC 註冊（揭露成本高）。私募——與特定買方議價（如壽險公司、共同基金），不需 SEC 註冊或承銷商。
  • Secondary market (次級市場): one owner sells to another; the firm gets no money, but secondary-market liquidity makes investors more willing to buy in the primary market.次級市場：投資人之間互相買賣，公司不收取資金；但次級市場的流動性讓投資人更願意在初級市場認購。

Chapter 2 · Financial Statements: Balance Sheet & Income Statement第 2 章 · 財務報表：資產負債表與損益表

2.1 The Balance Sheet 資產負債表

  • A snapshot of assets and liabilities at a point in time ("as of …"). Assets on the left, in order of decreasing liquidity; liabilities and equity on the right, in order of when due.某時點資產與負債的快照。左邊資產依流動性由高到低；右邊負債與權益依到期先後排列。
  • Fixed assets: long life — tangible (truck, computer) or intangible (trademark, patent). Current assets: life < 1 year — cash, accounts receivable (owed by customers), inventory.固定資產：壽命長——有形（卡車、電腦）或無形（商標、專利）。流動資產：一年內——現金、應收帳款（客戶欠款）、存貨。
  • Current liabilities: due within the year (accounts payable to suppliers). Long-term liabilities: not due in the coming year (e.g., a 5-year loan; "bonds" = long-term debt).流動負債：一年內須支付（應付供應商帳款）。長期負債：非一年內到期（如五年期貸款；「債券」即長期負債）。
  • Shareholders' equity: the residual value left to shareholders if the firm sold all assets and paid off its debts.股東權益：若公司賣掉所有資產並還清負債，剩下屬於股東的殘餘價值。
Balance Sheet Identity 資產負債表恆等式
Assets = Liabilities + Stockholders' Equity
Intuition 直覺：It always "balances" because equity is defined as the residual: Equity = Assets − Liabilities.
永遠平衡，因為權益本身就定義為剩餘：權益 = 資產 − 負債。
Net Working Capital 淨營運資金
NWC = Current Assets − Current Liabilities
Intuition 直覺：Usually positive for a healthy firm — cash available over the next 12 months exceeds cash that must be paid.
健康公司通常為正——未來 12 個月可得現金大於必須支付的現金。

2.2 Three Things to Watch on the Balance Sheet 看資產負債表的三個重點

  • 1. Liquidity: speed and ease of conversion to cash without significant loss of value. Liquid: cash, A/R, inventory, gold, stocks, government bonds. Illiquid: buildings, custom facilities, trademarks. Trade-off: liquidity lowers financial-distress risk, but liquid assets are less profitable.1. 流動性：轉換為現金的速度與容易程度，且不大幅減損價值。高流動：現金、應收、存貨、黃金、股票、公債；低流動：建築物、客製化廠房、商標。取捨：流動性越高財務風險越小，但獲利越低。
  • 2. Debt vs equity: equity holders have only a residual claim (paid after creditors). Using debt = financial leverage: it increases potential reward to shareholders but also the risk of financial distress and failure.2. 負債 vs 權益：股東只有剩餘請求權（債權人優先）。使用負債即財務槓桿：提高股東潛在報酬，但也提高財務危機與破產風險。
  • 3. Market vs book value: book value = historical cost minus accumulated depreciation (GAAP); market value = price at which assets can actually be bought or sold. Many valuable assets (good management, reputation, talented employees, brand) are not on the balance sheet. Financial managers care about the market value of the stock.3. 市價 vs 帳面價值：帳面價值＝歷史成本減累計折舊（GAAP）；市價＝實際可買賣的價格。許多有價值的資產（好管理、商譽、人才、品牌）不在資產負債表上。財務經理關心的是股票的市價。
Example 2.2 · Klingon Corporation (Market vs Book)
Book: CA $400 + FA $700 = $1,100; LTD $500 → Equity = $600. Market: CA $600 + FA $1,000 = $1,600; LTD $500 → Equity = $1,100. Market equity is almost twice book equity. 帳面：流動 400 + 固定 700 = 1,100，長期負債 500 → 權益 600。市價：600 + 1,000 = 1,600，負債 500 → 權益 1,100。市價權益約為帳面的兩倍。

2.3 The Income Statement 損益表

  • Measures performance over a period (quarter or year) — a "video recording" between two balance-sheet snapshots. Order: revenue → operating expenses → financing expenses (interest) → taxes → net income ("bottom line").衡量一段期間（季或年）的績效——兩張資產負債表快照之間的「錄影」。順序：收入 → 營業費用 → 融資費用（利息）→ 稅 → 淨利（最後一行）。
Income Statement Equation 損益表方程式
Net income = Revenue − Expenses
NI = Dividends + Addition to retained earnings
EBITEarnings before interest and taxes · 息前稅前盈餘
EPSNet income per share · 每股盈餘
ΔREAdded to cumulative retained earnings on the balance sheet · 加入資產負債表的累積保留盈餘

2.4 Three Things to Watch on the Income Statement 看損益表的三個重點

  • 1. GAAP: Recognition — revenue is recognized when fully earned (at the time of sale), not when cash is collected. Matching — costs of producing a sale are recognized together with that revenue, even if cash flowed at different times.1. GAAP：收入認列——賺得時（銷售時）認列，而非收到現金時。配合原則——產生收入的成本與收入同時認列，即使現金流出時點不同。
  • 2. Noncash items: the main one is depreciation. Buy a $5,000 asset for cash, depreciate straight-line over 5 years → $1,000 expense per year, but the only cash outflow happened at purchase. Example: Bed Bath & Beyond (Jan 2021) reported a net loss of $1.8 million yet positive cash flow of $244 million.2. 非現金項目：最主要是折舊。以現金買 $5,000 資產、直線法 5 年折舊 → 每年費用 $1,000，但實際現金流出只發生在購買時。例：Bed Bath & Beyond（2021 年 1 月）淨損 180 萬美元，現金流量卻為正 2.44 億美元。
  • 3. Time and costs: in the long run all costs are variable; in the short run some are fixed (property taxes, depreciation) and some variable (wages, supplier payments). Product costs (raw materials, direct labor, overhead) → COGS. Period costs → SG&A (e.g., the president's salary).3. 時間與成本：長期所有成本皆變動；短期有固定成本（財產稅、折舊）與變動成本（工資、供應商貨款）。產品成本（原料、直接人工、製造費用）→ 銷貨成本。期間成本 → 銷管費用（如總經理薪水）。
  • Earnings management: firms have discretion to over- or understate earnings to smooth dips and surges — a controversial practice. GAAP and IFRS are similar but not fully converged.盈餘管理：公司可酌情高估或低估盈餘以平滑波動——具爭議性。GAAP 與 IFRS 相似但尚未完全接軌。

4.1 Time Value of Money 貨幣的時間價值

  • A dollar in hand today is worth more than a dollar promised in the future, because today's dollar can earn interest while you wait. The trade-off depends on the rate you can earn by investing (the opportunity cost).今天的一元比未來承諾的一元更有價值，因為今天的錢可以賺利息。兩者的取捨取決於你的投資報酬率（機會成本）。
  • PowerBall example: a $110 million jackpot paid as $5.5 million a year for 20 years is worth much less than $110 million today.威力彩例子：1.1 億美元頭獎分 20 年、每年付 550 萬，今天的價值遠低於 1.1 億。

4.2 Future Value and Compounding 終值與複利

  • Future value (FV): the amount an investment grows to over time at a given rate. One period: $100 at 10% → $100 × 1.1 = $110.終值：投資在某利率下經過一段時間成長到的金額。一期：$100 × 1.1 = $110。
  • Two periods: $110 × 1.1 = $121, made of four parts: principal $100 + year-1 interest $10 + year-2 interest on principal $10 + interest on interest $1.兩期：$110 × 1.1 = $121，包含四部分：本金 $100＋第一年利息 $10＋第二年本金利息 $10＋利上加利 $1。
  • Compounding = reinvesting the interest, earning interest on interest (compound interest). Simple interest = interest earned only on the original principal; interest is not reinvested.複利＝利息再投資，賺取利上加利。單利＝只對原始本金計息，利息不再投資。
Future Value 終值
FV = PV × (1 + r)t
PVPrincipal invested today · 今天投入的本金（現值）
rInterest rate per period · 每期利率
tNumber of periods · 期數
(1+r)tFuture value interest factor, FVIF(r, t) · 終值因子
Intuition 直覺：For a given r, FV rises as t rises; for a given t, FV rises as r rises. Compounding is small over short horizons but huge over long ones.
r 固定時，t 越長 FV 越大；t 固定時，r 越高 FV 越大。複利效果短期不明顯，長期非常驚人。

4.3 Present Value and Discounting 現值與折現

Present Value (basic PV equation) 現值（基本現值方程式）
PV = FVt / (1 + r)t  ⇔  PV × (1 + r)t = FVt
FVtCash to be received after t periods · t 期後收到的金額
rDiscount rate · 折現率
1/(1+r)tDiscount factor = PV interest factor, PVIF(r, t); the reciprocal of the FV factor · 折現因子（現值因子），為終值因子的倒數
Intuition 直覺：PV is the reverse of FV — we discount back instead of compounding forward. Finding the PV of future cash flows is discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation. For a given r, longer t → lower PV; for a given t, higher r → lower PV (PV and discount rates are inversely related).
現值是終值的反向——把錢折回今天而非複利到未來。計算未來現金流量現值即現金流量折現 (DCF) 評價。r 固定時 t 越長 PV 越低；t 固定時 r 越高 PV 越低（現值與折現率反向）。

4.4 Evaluating Investments: PV vs FV 評估投資：比較現值與終值

Example 4.7 · Buy an asset for $335, sell for $400 in 3 years; alternative earns 10%
Method 1 (compare FVs): $335 × 1.13 = $445.89 > $400 → the alternative is better.
Method 2 (compare PVs): $400 / 1.13 = $300.53 < $335 → you only need about $300 to get $400; reject the asset.
The 10% is the opportunity cost. 方法一（比終值）：$335 × 1.13 = $445.89 > $400 → 其他投資較好。
方法二（比現值）：$400 / 1.13 = $300.53 < $335 → 只需約 $300 即可得到 $400，不應投資。
10% 即機會成本。

5.1 Future Value of Multiple Cash Flows 多筆現金流量的終值

FV of Multiple Cash Flows 多筆現金流量終值

5.3 Annuities 年金

  • Ordinary annuity: a series of constant (level) cash flows occurring at the end of each period for a fixed number of periods. Car loans and home mortgages are typical examples.普通年金：固定期數內、每期期末發生的等額現金流量。車貸、房貸是典型例子。
Present Value of an Ordinary Annuity 普通年金現值
Annuity PV = C × [1 − 1/(1 + r)t] / r = C × (1 − PV factor) / r
CLevel payment per period · 每期等額支付
rRate per period · 每期利率
tNumber of payments · 支付期數
PVIFA(r,t)[1 − 1/(1+r)t]/r = annuity present value factor · 年金現值因子
Derivation (slides) 推導：Sum of a geometric series = first term × (1 − ration)/(1 − ratio). First term = C(1+r)−1, ratio = 1/(1+r) → C(1+r)−1 × [1 − 1/(1+r)t] / [r/(1+r)] = C × [1 − 1/(1+r)t]/r.
等比級數和 = 首項 × (1 − 公比n)/(1 − 公比)。首項 C(1+r)−1、公比 1/(1+r)，化簡即得公式。
Slide Examples 投影片範例
• $500 at the end of each of 3 years, 10%: one by one 454.55 + 413.22 + 375.66 = $1,243.43; or factor = (1 − 1/1.331)/0.1 = 2.48685 → 500 × 2.48685 = $1,243.43. Calculator: 3 N, 10 I/Y, 500 PMT, CPT PV = −1,243.43.
• Finding C (Yakee Doodle Dandy): borrow $100,000, 5 equal annual payments, 18%: factor = (1 − 1/1.185)/0.18 = 3.1272 → C = 100,000 / 3.1272 = $31,977.78.

Annuity Due 期初年金
Annuity due value = Ordinary annuity value × (1 + r)
Intuition 直覺：Cash flows occur at the beginning of each period (e.g., apartment lease — first payment due immediately). Each payment arrives one period earlier, so its value is (1 + r) times higher. Works for both PV and FV.
現金流量在每期期初發生（如租屋——第一筆租金立即支付）。每筆提早一期，價值乘以 (1 + r)。現值與終值皆適用。

5.5 Comparing Rates: EAR and APR 利率比較：有效年利率與年百分率

  • 10% compounded semiannually = 5% every six months: $1 × 1.052 = $1.1025 → an effective annual rate (EAR) of 10.25%. The 10% is the stated (quoted) rate. The extra $0.0025 is 5% interest earned on the first 5 cents.10% 半年複利 = 每半年 5%：$1 × 1.052 = $1.1025 → 有效年利率 (EAR) 10.25%。10% 是報價（名目）利率。多出的 $0.0025 是前半年 5 分錢再賺 5% 的利息。
Effective Annual Rate 有效年利率
EAR = (1 + Quoted rate / m)m − 1
Quoted rateStated annual rate (APR) · 報價年利率
mTimes compounded per year · 每年複利次數
Three steps 三步驟：(1) divide the quoted rate by m; (2) add 1 and raise to the power m; (3) subtract 1. The highest quoted rate is not necessarily the best — compare EARs.
(1) 報價利率除以 m；(2) 加 1 後取 m 次方；(3) 減 1。報價最高不一定最好——要比較 EAR。
Annual Percentage Rate 年百分率 APR
APR = Rate per period × Periods per year
Intuition 直覺：Required on all consumer loans by U.S. truth-in-lending laws; the APR is a quoted rate, so the true cost is the EAR. E.g., 1% per month → APR 12%, EAR = 1.0112 − 1 = 12.6825%.
美國誠實借貸法要求所有消費性貸款揭露 APR；APR 是報價利率，真正成本是 EAR。例：月利 1% → APR 12%，EAR = 12.6825%。

5.6 Loan Types and Loan Amortization 貸款類型與攤還

  • Pure discount loan: receive money today, repay a single lump sum later; common for terms of a year or less. Lend against $25,000 due in 5 years at 12% → PV = 25,000 / 1.7623 = $14,186.純折價貸款：今天取得資金，未來一次償還；常見於一年以內的貸款。5 年後還 $25,000、12% → 可借 $14,186。

6.1 Bond Basics 債券基本概念

  • A bond is the debt security a corporation or government sells to borrow long-term from the public. It is normally an interest-only loan: interest every period, all principal repaid at the end. Bonds are valued by discounted cash flow.債券是公司或政府向大眾長期借款所發行的債務證券，通常是只付息貸款：每期付息、到期一次還本。以現金流量折現法評價。
  • Beck Corporation example: borrow $1,000 for 30 years at 12% → pays $120 a year (the coupon) for 30 years. A constant annual coupon = level coupon bond.Beck 公司例子：借 $1,000、30 年、12% → 每年付 $120（票息），共 30 年。每年固定票息＝固定票息債券。
  • Face value / par value (面額): the principal repaid at maturity, usually $1,000 for corporate bonds (government bonds have larger par values). A bond selling at par is a par value bond.面額：到期償還的本金，公司債通常為 $1,000（公債面額較大）。以面額出售的債券稱平價債券。
  • Coupon rate (票面利率) = annual coupon / face value = $120/$1,000 = 12%. Maturity (到期) = years until face value is paid; it declines after issuance.票面利率 = 年票息 / 面額 = 12%。到期期間 = 距支付面額的年數，發行後逐年減少。
  • Yield to maturity (YTM, 殖利率): the market's required rate on a bond. Note: the "market interest rate" is the general current rate (set by macro factors and central-bank policy); YTM is the return implied by a specific bond's market price. The average YTM of many similar-maturity bonds can proxy for the market rate.殖利率 (YTM)：市場對該債券要求的報酬率。註：「市場利率」泛指目前利率水準（受總經與央行政策影響）；YTM 是特定債券市價所隱含的報酬率。可用許多相近到期日債券 YTM 的平均值作為市場利率指標。

6.3 Interest Rate Risk 利率風險

  • Interest rate risk: the risk to bondholders from fluctuating interest rates. It depends on how sensitive the price is to rate changes, which depends on two things, all else equal:利率風險：利率波動帶給債券持有人的風險，取決於價格對利率的敏感度，在其他條件相同下由兩個因素決定：
  • ① Longer time to maturity → greater interest rate risk. ② Lower coupon rate → greater interest rate risk.① 到期期間越長 → 利率風險越大。 ② 票面利率越低 → 利率風險越大。
  • Why (Figure 6.2): the 30-year bond's price line is much steeper than the 1-year bond's. A large part of a bond's value is the $1,000 face amount; discounted over one year, a small rate change barely affects it, but compounded over 30 years a small rate change has a big effect on PV.原因（圖 6.2）：30 年期債券的價格曲線比 1 年期陡得多。債券價值有很大部分來自 $1,000 面額；折現一年時利率小變動影響不大，但折現 30 年時利率小變動會大幅影響現值。

6.5 Debt vs Equity 負債與權益的差異

  • Creditor (債權人) = lender; debtor (債務人) = borrower.債權人＝放款人；債務人＝借款人。
  • Debt is not an ownership interest; creditors generally have no voting power.負債不是所有權；債權人通常沒有投票權。
  • Interest is a cost of doing business and is fully tax deductible; dividends are not tax deductible.利息是經營成本，可全額抵稅；股利不可抵稅。
  • Unpaid debt is a liability; creditors can legally claim the firm's assets → possible liquidation or reorganization (bankruptcy). The possibility of financial failure is a cost of debt that equity does not create.未付清的債是負債；債權人可依法對公司資產求償 → 可能清算或重整（破產）。財務失敗的可能性是發行負債的成本，發行股票則無此問題。
  • Gray areas: perpetual bonds paying interest only if earned, hybrid securities, preferred stock. General rule: equity = ownership and residual claim (paid after debt holders). Debt's maximum reward is fixed by the loan; equity's upside is unlimited.灰色地帶：僅在有盈餘時付息的永續債、混合型證券、特別股。原則：權益＝所有權且為剩餘請求權（在債權人之後受償）。債權的最大報酬固定，股權的報酬沒有上限。

6.6 Long-Term Debt Features 長期負債的特徵

  • Maturity: short-term debt ≤ 1 year; long-term > 1 year.到期期間：短期 ≤ 1 年；長期 > 1 年。
  • Bond vs debenture vs note: strictly, a bond is secured debt, a debenture is unsecured debt, and a note is debt with a shorter maturity (unsecured, usually < ~10 years at issue).債券、信用債券、票據：嚴格來說，bond 為有擔保債務，debenture 為無擔保債務，note 為到期較短的債務（無擔保，發行時通常不到約 10 年）。
  • Public vs private issue: private debt is placed directly with a lender and not offered to the public; terms are up to the parties.公開發行 vs 私募：私募債直接配售給貸方，不對大眾發行，條款由雙方議定。
  • Other dimensions: security, call features, sinking funds, ratings, protective covenants.其他面向：擔保、贖回條款、償債基金、信用評等、保護條款。

6.7 The Indenture 債券契約

  • The indenture (deed of trust) is the written agreement between the corporation and its creditors. A trustee (e.g., a bank) represents the bondholders: makes sure the terms are obeyed, manages the sinking fund (if any), and represents bondholders in default.債券契約（信託契據）是公司與債權人之間的書面協議。受託人（如銀行）代表債券持有人：確保條款被遵守、管理償債基金（若有）、在違約時代表持有人。
  • Provisions: basic terms, total amount issued, property used as security, repayment arrangements, call provisions, protective covenants.條款內容：基本條件、發行總額、擔保財產、償還安排、贖回條款、保護條款。
  • Face value: historically $1,000 (also $2,000, e.g., Alphabet); municipal bonds often $5,000; Treasury bonds $10,000 or $100,000. Also called principal value.面額：傳統為 $1,000（$2,000 也常見，如 Alphabet）；市政債常為 $5,000；美國公債 $10,000 或 $100,000。又稱本金價值。
  • Registered form (registrar records ownership) vs bearer form (whoever holds the certificate is paid). Bearer bonds' drawbacks: hard to recover if lost or stolen; the firm cannot notify unknown owners. Now much less common in the U.S.記名債券（由登記機構記錄所有權）vs 無記名債券（持有憑證者即受償）。無記名缺點：遺失或被竊難以追回；公司不知持有人，無法通知重大事件。現在美國已少見。

6.8 Security and Seniority 擔保與受償順位

  • Collateral (擔保品): securities (bonds, stocks) pledged for payment; collateral trust bonds often pledge common stock held by the corporation.擔保品：質押作為還款保證的證券（債券、股票）；擔保信託債券常以公司持有的普通股質押。
  • Mortgage securities: secured by real property (land, buildings); described in a mortgage trust indenture. A blanket mortgage pledges all the company's real property.不動產抵押債券：以不動產（土地、建物）擔保；載於抵押信託契約。概括抵押以公司全部不動產擔保。
  • Unsecured — debenture: no specific pledge; holders claim only property not otherwise pledged. Today almost all U.S. public industrial and financial bonds are debentures, while most utility and railroad bonds are secured.無擔保——信用債券：無特定擔保；只能對未被質押的剩餘財產求償。目前美國工業與金融公司的公開債券幾乎都是信用債券，而公用事業與鐵路債券多有擔保。
  • Seniority (優先順序): senior vs junior debt. Subordinated debt (次順位) is paid only after specified creditors in default — but debt cannot be subordinated to equity.受償順位：優先債 vs 次級債。違約時次順位債務在特定債權人受償後才輪到——但債務不能次於股權。

6.9 Repayment, Call Provision and Covenants 償還、贖回條款與保護條款

  • Sinking fund: an account managed by the trustee to repay bonds early; the firm pays the trustee annually, and the trustee retires debt by buying bonds in the market or calling a fraction. Variations: start ~10 years after issue; equal payments; or insufficient payments leaving a large balloon payment at maturity.償債基金：由受託人管理、用於提前償還債券的帳戶；公司每年付款給受託人，受託人於市場買回或部分贖回。形式多樣：發行約 10 年後開始；每年等額；或提撥不足、到期需支付大額氣球式付款。
  • Call provision (贖回條款): lets the firm repurchase bonds at stated prices over a period. Call premium = call price − par value. A deferred call provision prohibits calling for the first years (e.g., 10); during that time the bond is call protected.贖回條款：允許公司在一定期間以事先約定價格買回債券。贖回溢價 = 贖回價 − 面額。遞延贖回條款禁止前幾年（如 10 年）贖回，此期間債券受贖回保護。
  • Negative covenants (limit actions): limit dividends; no pledging assets to other lenders; no mergers; no selling/leasing major assets without approval; no additional long-term debt.消極條款（限制行為）：限制股利；不得將資產質押給其他貸方；不得合併；未經同意不得出售或出租主要資產；不得再發行長期負債。
  • Positive covenants (required actions): keep working capital above a minimum; periodically provide audited financial statements; maintain collateral in good condition.積極條款（必須做到）：維持營運資金高於最低水準；定期提供查核後財報；維護擔保品狀況良好。

6.10 Bond Ratings 信用評等

  • Leading raters: Moody's and Standard & Poor's (S&P). Ratings assess creditworthiness: how likely the firm is to default and creditors' protection if it does.主要評等機構：穆迪與標準普爾。評等衡量信用品質：違約可能性以及違約時債權人的保障。
  • Ratings address only default risk, not interest rate risk — a highly rated bond's price can still be very volatile.評等只考慮違約風險，不考慮利率風險——高評等債券的價格仍可能大幅波動。
  • AAA/Aaa = highest quality, lowest default risk (early 2021: only Johnson & Johnson and Microsoft among U.S. nonfinancial firms). AA/Aa = very good, more common. D = in default.AAA/Aaa = 最高品質、違約風險最低（2021 年初美國非金融公司僅嬌生與微軟）。AA/Aa = 非常好，較常見。D = 已違約。
  • Investment grade = at least BBB (S&P) or Baa (Moody's). Below that = junk bonds (low-grade, high-yield 高收益債).投資等級 = 至少 BBB（標普）或 Baa（穆迪）。低於此為垃圾債券（低等級，又稱高收益債）。
  • Fallen angels: bonds downgraded from investment grade into junk, e.g., Moody's downgraded Pemex (Jan 2021); the 2020 slowdown turned over $370 billion of U.S. corporate debt into fallen angels.墮落天使：從投資等級被降為垃圾等級的債券，如穆迪於 2021 年 1 月調降墨西哥國營石油公司 Pemex；2020 年經濟放緩使逾 3,700 億美元美國公司債成為墮落天使。
  • Default rates (Figure 6.3): about 30% of CCC/C bonds default within 1 year and 50% within 9 years; only about 1–2% of AAA/AA bonds within 20 years. After 10 years, BB bonds default more than twice as often as BBB — showing why investment grade matters.違約率（圖 6.3）：CCC/C 債券約 30% 在 1 年內違約、50% 在 9 年內違約；AAA/AA 在 20 年內僅約 1–2%。10 年後 BB 的違約率是 BBB 的兩倍以上——可見投資等級的重要性。

Exercise 5Ch 6 · Bonds and Bond Valuation 債券評價

Q27
To borrow money from the public on a long-term basis, a corporation should:
Correct answer 正解：A
Long-term public borrowing is done by issuing debt securities, generically called bonds. Equity is not borrowing; a bank loan is not from the public.向大眾長期借款是發行債務證券（統稱債券）。發行股票不是借款；銀行貸款不是向大眾借。
Q28
Which information will not be displayed in a bond's indenture?
Correct answer 正解：A
The indenture fixes the bond's terms (coupon, face value, maturity, security, call provisions, covenants). YTM is set by the market and changes with the bond's price, so it is not in the contract.債券契約載明固定條款（票息、面額、到期日、擔保、贖回條款、保護條款）。殖利率由市場決定、隨價格變動，不在契約中。
Q29
When market interest rates rise, a par value bond could become:
Correct answer 正解：A
Bond prices and rates move in opposite directions. When rates rise above the coupon rate, the price falls below face value → discount bond (Xanth: $1,000 → $884.82).債券價格與利率反向。利率升到高於票面利率時，價格跌破面額 → 折價債券（Xanth：$1,000 → $884.82）。
Q30
Which bond suffers from greater interest rate risk?
Correct answer 正解：A
All else equal, longer maturity and lower coupon rate mean greater interest rate risk.其他條件相同，到期期間越長、票面利率越低，利率風險越大。
Q31
